03 · Facilities Management

Facilities Management

Beyond individual units, AG Emerald keeps the buildings and estates our clients own running smoothly — administering service charges with full transparency and keeping shared spaces safe, clean and well maintained.

Service Charge Administration

  • Annual budget preparation & owner sign-off
  • Levying, invoicing & collection follow-up
  • Transparent quarterly financial reporting
  • Reconciliation, arrears management & audit support
  • Utility & vendor payment coordination

Common Area Management

  • Cleaning, landscaping & refuse management oversight
  • Security personnel & access-control coordination
  • Maintenance of lifts, generators, lighting & fittings
  • Health, safety & compliance inspections
  • Enforcement of estate rules & house policies

Why It Matters

Well-run common areas and clean service charge accounts protect property value and owner trust alike. Our facilities team gives boards, landlords and residents' associations a single accountable partner for the upkeep, compliance and finances of the spaces everyone shares.
