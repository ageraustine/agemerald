Our Services

Everything a property owner or tenant needs, under one roof.

From finding and closing a sale to managing the day-to-day and keeping the books straight — AG Emerald runs the full lifecycle of a property so you don't have to coordinate three different firms to do it.

01

Sales & Acquisitions

End-to-end support for buying or selling — market appraisal, listing and marketing, qualified buyer introductions, negotiation, and coordination with legal teams through to transfer. Whether you're a first-time buyer or disposing of a portfolio asset, we manage the process so you close with confidence.

02

Rentals & Property Management

Full-service leasing and day-to-day management for landlords and tenants — marketing, tenant screening, rent collection, lease administration and maintenance coordination, backed by technology that keeps owners informed. We let both furnished and unfurnished units, so tenants moving in with their own furniture and those wanting a move-in-ready home are equally well served.

03

Accounting & Financial Reporting

Transparent service-charge and rental accounting for owners, residents' associations and investors — clear quarterly statements, arrears follow-up, and financial reporting that turns a black box into a report you can actually read.

How We Work

One point of contact, start to finish.

You get a single accountable team across search, transaction, letting and management — rather than handing a property off between separate agents, managers and accountants. It means faster answers, fewer handoffs, and a consistent standard of service at every stage.
