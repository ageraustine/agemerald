Property Types

Whatever the asset class, we know the market for it.

From a one-bedroom apartment to a commercial block or a parcel of investment land, AG Emerald brings the same depth of market knowledge and the same single-point-of-contact service to every property type we handle.

Residential

Apartments, townhouses and standalone homes for sale or rent — furnished and unfurnished — across Nairobi's most sought-after neighbourhoods.

Commercial Offices

Office suites and full floors for businesses of every size, with lease structuring and tenant-improvement coordination handled end to end.

Retail, Mixed-Use & Investment Land

Retail units in established shopping centres, mixed-use developments combining residential and commercial space, and investment land with strong appreciation or development potential — sourced, appraised and transacted with the same rigour as any built asset.

Industrial & Warehousing

Warehouse and light-industrial space for logistics, storage and manufacturing tenants, matched to access, power and loading requirements.

Managed Estates

Gated residential estates and apartment blocks under full facilities and service-charge management, with transparent reporting to residents.
